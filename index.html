Step 02 • Core Concept

Understanding SparkX & Modules

SparkX operates as an Employee Self-Service (ESS) platform tailored for clarity and efficiency.

What is Employee Self-Service (ESS)?

Instead of emailing HR or filing paper slips for everyday administrative needs, ESS empowers every employee to view, manage, and request company services autonomously in real-time.

Pathway 1: Employee Invitation

Employee Invitation

Audience: Employees invited by their company's HR or Administrator

If your employer is already set up on SparkX, you do NOT create a separate company organization. Your HR administrator initiates your account invitation directly.

Organization Creation

Admin Account Journey

Create an Admin Account & Register Your Organization

Follow these steps in order to create your SparkX admin account, register your organization, and wait for Super Admin approval.

1

Step 1: Create Your Account

Open the SparkX registration page and complete the initial account form using your name and email address. Select Continue to proceed. Make sure you use an email address that you can access, because SparkX will send a confirmation email to this address.

/register — Create your account
Complete the initial registration form with your name and email address.
2

Step 2: Confirm Your Email Address

After submitting the account form, a confirmation screen will ask you to check your inbox. Open the confirmation email sent by SparkX and select the confirmation link to verify your email address.

Registration — Check your inbox
After submitting your details, SparkX displays a reminder to check your email.
Email — Confirm your email
Open the email from SparkX and use the confirmation link to verify your email address.
3

Step 3: Create Your Password

Once your email address has been confirmed, continue to the password setup page. Create a password for your SparkX account and submit it to complete your account setup.

/create-password
Create a password to secure your SparkX admin account.
4

Step 4: Choose Create an Organization

After signing in, SparkX will show the available options. Since you are registering a new company, select Create a new organization to continue with the organization registration process. If you are joining an existing employer, choose the employee invitation option instead.

/start — Choose how to continue
Select Create a new organization to register your company on SparkX.
5

Step 5: Fill In Your Organization Details

Complete the organization registration form with the required company information. Review the details carefully, then select Submit for approval. A currency confirmation dialog will appear; check the selected currency and confirm that it is correct before submitting your request.

/organization/create — Organization details
Enter the required company details in the organization registration form.
Confirmation dialog — Company currency
Review and confirm the currency selected for your organization before submitting.
6

Step 6: Wait for Super Admin Approval

After your organization details have been submitted, the request will be reviewed by the Super Admin. Your SparkX page will show that the request is waiting for approval, and you will also receive an email confirming that your request has been received. Wait for the approval decision before proceeding.

/start — Waiting for approval
SparkX displays the organization request status while it is being reviewed.
Email — Request received
You will receive an email confirming that SparkX has received your organization request.
REJECTION SCENARIOWhat happens if the request is rejected?The Super Admin may reject the request after reviewing the submitted organization details.

If your request is rejected, check the notification and the message shown in SparkX for further information. You will need to address the issue before continuing with organization setup.

7

Step 7: Receive Approval and Continue

If the Super Admin approves your organization request, you will receive an approval email confirming that your organization is ready. Return to SparkX and continue from the organization page. You can then proceed to the next section of this guideline to set up your organization.

Setting Up Your Organization

A comprehensive, step-by-step administrative guide for configuring your company's workspace after Super Admin approval.

Prerequisites: The organization setup wizard is accessible only AFTER: 1. Account creation; 2. Email verification; 3. Super Admin approval; and 4. Successful login.

Step 1: Organization Profile

Step 1 of 8
Purpose
Establish the legal entity, country, base currency, and time zone for the organization.
What You Need to Prepare
Company Legal Name, Short Code (e.g. MJL), NPWP / Reg. no., and Company Timezone.

Instructions:

  • 1Verify the pre-filled Organization Name (e.g., ABC Group) and unique system-assigned organization code.
  • 2Enter the First Company (legal name) and define a concise Short code (e.g. ABC).
  • 3Input the official NPWP / Reg. no. for corporate compliance.
  • 4Confirm the Country (e.g. Indonesia) and select your operational Time zone (e.g. Asia/Jakarta GMT+7).
  • 5Click Save & continue to proceed to module configuration.

Step 3: Job Levels

Step 3 of 8
Purpose
Establish corporate hierarchy layers (Direktur, Manager, HR, Staff) and specific levels used for policy limits.
What You Need to Prepare
Organizational job leveling structure and seniority hierarchy.

Instructions:

4

Step 4: Operational Policies

Step 4 of 8
Purpose
Configure organizational rules across 5 operational pillars: Leave, Claim & Expenses, Travel, Overtime, and Approval Routing.
What You Need to Prepare
Leave allowances (Annual, Sick, Maternity), receipt attachment thresholds, travel per diems, overtime multipliers, and approval chains.

Instructions across 5 Sub-Tabs:

  • 1Leave Sub-tab: Configure annual allowances, carryover limits, advance notice days, and add custom leave types via "+ Add leave type".
  • 2Claim & Expenses Sub-tab: Set mandatory receipt attachment requirements and operational categories (Meals, Transport, Accommodation, Parking, Groceries, Supplies, Other).
  • 3Travel Sub-tab: Define domestic/international daily per diems and accommodation booking standards.
  • 4Overtime Sub-tab: Set job level eligibility, workday vs holiday multipliers, and minimum request duration.
  • 5Approval Routing Sub-tab: Establish approval tiers (Direct Manager -> Department Head -> HR -> Finance) for each request type.

Policy menu overview

Use these menu categories to navigate the policy settings. Each category can be configured separately.

Leaves Claim & Expenses Travel Overtime Approval Routing

1. Leaves

Open the Leaves menu to review leave types and configure their allowances and rules. Use the available add, edit, or remove controls as needed, then save the settings.

2. Claim & Expenses

Open Claim & Expenses to configure expense settings, categories, and the rules employees must follow when submitting claims.

3. Travel

Open Travel to configure travel-related policy settings, accommodation requirements, and supporting details.

4. Overtime

Open Overtime to review overtime eligibility and configure the applicable overtime rules and limits.

5. Approval Routing

Open Approval Routing to set up approval routes and templates for the relevant request types. Review each route before completing the policy configuration.

Important Notes:

Approval routing allows multi-tier hierarchies (e.g. Direct Manager -> Dept Head -> HR -> Finance) for each request type.

Step 5: Limits & Allowances (Mandatory Gateway)

Step 5 of 8 • Critical Gate
Purpose
Set financial spending caps and travel booking entitlements per job level for expenses and business trips.
What You Need to Prepare
Category caps (Meals, Transport, Accommodation, Parking, Groceries, Supplies) and allowed flight/train classes.

Instructions across 2 Sub-Tabs:

  • 1Claim Limits Matrix: For every job level (Direktur, Manager, HR, Staff), enter exact monetary caps or check "No limit" across all expense categories.
  • 2Travel Limits Matrix: Input daily per diem allowances and hotel night caps for each level.
  • 3Transportation Classes: Open class selection popovers to tick allowed flight classes (Economy, Business, etc.) and train classes (Economy, Executive) per level.
  • 4Click Continue to proceed to Structure.
